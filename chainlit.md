# Welcome to Mindrian

**Your AI-Powered Innovation Workshop Platform**

Mindrian helps you identify and solve **Problems Worth Solving (PWS)** — a proven methodology from Johns Hopkins University for validating ideas before investing time and resources into solutions that may not address real needs.

---

## What is PWS?

**Problems Worth Solving** is a structured approach to innovation that ensures you're working on problems that matter. Instead of jumping to solutions, PWS helps you:

- Clarify what the **real problem** is (not symptoms)
- Validate that the problem **actually exists** with evidence
- Understand **who experiences** the problem and how
- Determine if **solving it matters** (impact, urgency, willingness to pay)

## What to Expect

**Mindrian will ask you questions.** Unlike AI tools that jump to answers, PWS methodology requires understanding before solving. Expect to:

- Be challenged on assumptions
- Provide evidence for claims
- Think through implications
- Consider multiple perspectives

**After 8+ turns of exploration**, if you're ready for a direct answer, hit the **Give me your answer** button — no guilt, no "are you sure?"

**Every few turns**, you may get a cross-domain insight — a connection from an unexpected field that sparks new thinking.

**Your context is preserved.** Switch between workshops anytime — your conversation history carries over. Close your browser and come back later; you'll get a recap of where you left off.

## FAQ

**Q: Which workshop should I start with?**
Start with **Lawrence** to clarify your problem. He'll suggest other workshops when appropriate. Or try **PWS Consultant** for a structured diagnostic flow.

**Q: How long does a workshop take?**
It depends on your problem's complexity. A quick clarification might take 10 minutes; a full analysis could be several sessions.

**Q: Can I export my work?**
Yes! Click **Synthesize & Download** to get a document of your session with key insights.

**Q: What if I don't have data yet?**
That's fine — PWS helps you identify *what* data you need. Use the **Research** button to gather evidence.

**Q: Is my conversation saved?**
Yes, your sessions are saved so you can continue later. Click the history icon to access past conversations.

**Q: Can I use voice?**
Yes! Click the microphone icon to speak. Responses can also be read aloud.

**Q: How do I get more detailed responses?**
Use the Settings gear (top-right) and increase the "Response Detail" slider.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Be specific** — "How do I grow my business?" is less helpful than "Why are enterprise customers churning after 3 months?"
- **Bring evidence** — Upload documents, share data, provide examples
- **Challenge yourself** — If the AI agrees too easily, try Red Teaming
- **Use multiple workshops** — Start with Lawrence, then go deeper with a specialized workshop
- **Iterate** — Great problem definitions often take multiple passes
